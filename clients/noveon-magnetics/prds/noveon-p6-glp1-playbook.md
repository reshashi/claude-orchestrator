> Generated by Planner on 2026-02-13

> Priority: HIGH

## 1. Executive Summary

GLP-1 drugs (Mounjaro, Ozempic, Wegovy) are the #1 cost driver for employers. Noveon's data proves it: Mounjaro went from $0 to $23,870 in 8 months. Every employer is experiencing this. This project creates a standardized GLP-1 audit template and playbook that turns one finding into a repeatable service offering. GLP-1s are the Trojan horse for employer engagement — every CFO is worried about this right now.

## 2. Goals & Success Criteria

- [ ] **GLP-1 Audit Template** — standardized analysis runnable on any employer's pharmacy data
- [ ] **Savings Calculator** — given X members on Y drugs at Z cost, compute savings from each intervention
- [ ] **Employer Education Materials** — "What you need to know about GLP-1s on your plan"
- [ ] **B2B Pitch Story** — the Noveon case study (anonymized) as a compelling sales narrative
- [ ] **Alternative Sourcing Matrix** — compounding, Canadian, manufacturer programs with cost comparisons
- [ ] **Clinical Criteria Checklist** — diabetes vs weight management, step therapy compliance
