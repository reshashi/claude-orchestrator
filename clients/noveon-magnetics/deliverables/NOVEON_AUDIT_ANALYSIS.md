## HIGH-VALUE AUDIT OPPORTUNITIES

### Opportunity #1: Breast Cancer Case - "Sarah" ($224K)

**This is the centerpiece finding and maps directly to the B2B pitch deck cancer story.**

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Member | F, Age 50-59, Employee (SB = subscriber) |
| Status | Active |
| ICD-10 | C50811 - Malignant neoplasm of overlapping sites of right female breast |
| Full Year Total (Jan-Dec 2025) | **$223,998** |
| Current Period Total (May-Dec 2025) | $147,868 |
| Medical Claims Paid | $191,308 (full year) / $113,993 (current) |
| Drug Claims | $226 (full year) / $191 (current) |
| Medical Specialty Rx | **$86,024** (oncology infusions) |
| Out of Network % | 4% |
| Clinical Programs | COM (Complex Case Mgmt), WI (Well Informed/Gaps in Care) |
| In Base Period? | YES (ongoing treatment) |

**Audit Opportunities on this case**:
1. **Medical Specialty Rx ($86K)**: Oncology infusions are the #1 target for medical bill review. Site-of-care optimization (hospital outpatient vs. home infusion vs. physician office) can save 30-60%.
2. **Facility billing review**: $191K in medical claims over 12 months - high likelihood of billing errors, duplicate charges, unbundling.
3. **100% of medical specialty Rx spend for the entire plan is cancer-related** (per slide 25: "Cancer 100%" for Medical SRx HCP/Home Infusion).
4. **Reference-based pricing**: Because they're self-insured, they could negotiate directly or use RBP for facility charges.

**Conservative savings estimate**: 10-20% on the $224K = **$22,400 - $44,800**

## PRODUCT FIT ANALYSIS

### Product A: Bill Audit Service
**Score: STRONG FIT**

Why:
- Active $224K cancer case with ongoing treatment (perfect for line-by-line bill review)
- $90K respiratory case with suspicious negative adjustments
- Specialty pharmacy spend concentrated in 3-4 high-cost members
- Low out-of-network usage (4-8%) means most claims are auditable
- Small enough that individual attention matters, large enough for meaningful savings

### Product B: Claims Data Platform
**Score: EXCELLENT FIT (the real prize)**

Why:
- **They're self-insured** - legal right to their own claims data under ERISA
- Pat may not even know this - educating him creates trust
- Current broker (Insurica) is providing standard Cigna reports, not proactive analytics
- GLP-1 trend is invisible to them without real-time claims monitoring
- 132 members = manageable data volume, meaningful insights
- Claims data would enable:
  - Real-time high-cost claim alerts
  - Pharmacy alternative recommendations (Canadian, compounding)
  - Provider cost comparison / reference-based pricing
  - Predictive modeling for next year's rates

### Consent Path to Claims Data
Since they're self-insured under ERISA:
1. The plan sponsor (Noveon) owns the data
2. Pat (as CFO / plan fiduciary) can authorize data sharing
3. Cigna is the TPA - they must provide claims data upon plan sponsor request
4. Everything in this report is de-identified, but with consent, they can get member-level data
5. A signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) would cover HIPAA requirements
