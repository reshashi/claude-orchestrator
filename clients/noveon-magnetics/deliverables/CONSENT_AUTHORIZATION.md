# Authorization for Release of Claims Data

## Plan Sponsor Authorization Under ERISA

**Employer/Plan Sponsor**: Noveon Magnetics
**Group Number**: 0632268
**Third-Party Administrator (TPA)**: Cigna
**Plan Type**: Level-Funded, Pooled Stop-Loss (Self-Insured under ERISA)

---

### Authorization

I, the undersigned, am an authorized representative of Noveon Magnetics ("Plan Sponsor") and a fiduciary of the Noveon Magnetics Employee Health Benefit Plan ("Plan"). In my capacity as Plan fiduciary, I hereby authorize and direct Cigna ("TPA") to release the following Plan data to MedicalBills.com ("Authorized Representative"):

### Data Requested

- [ ] **Claims data** — All medical and pharmacy claims paid on behalf of Plan members for the period of **January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2025** (or most recent available 12-month period)
- [ ] **Eligibility data** — Plan member eligibility records for the same period (de-identified as needed)
- [ ] **Plan documents** — Summary Plan Description (SPD), Schedule of Benefits, formulary

### Data Format

Claims data should be provided in one of the following formats (in order of preference):
1. EDI-835 (Electronic Remittance Advice)
2. CSV/Excel extract with standard claim fields
3. Cigna standard claims data extract

### Purpose

This data will be used exclusively for:
- Medical bill audit and claims analysis
- Identification of billing errors, overcharges, and optimization opportunities
- Generation of savings recommendations for the Plan Sponsor

### Legal Basis

As a self-insured plan under ERISA:
- The Plan Sponsor owns all claims data generated under the Plan
- The TPA is contractually obligated to provide claims data upon Plan Sponsor request
- This authorization is consistent with ERISA Section 104 reporting and disclosure requirements
- Release of claims data to an authorized representative does not require individual member consent when used for plan administration purposes

### HIPAA Compliance

MedicalBills.com agrees to:
- Execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Noveon Magnetics prior to receiving any Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Use minimum necessary standards in all data handling
- Maintain appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards
- Return or destroy all PHI upon completion of the engagement

### Duration

This authorization is effective from the date of signature through **12 months** from the date below, unless revoked earlier in writing.
