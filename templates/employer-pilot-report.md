### You're Self-Insured — And That Changes Everything

Your health plan is structured as a **[FUNDING_TYPE] arrangement with [TPA]** at a [FUNDING_PCT]% funding level with [STOP_LOSS_TYPE] coverage. In plain English: **you are self-insured**.

This means:
- **You own the claims data.** Under ERISA, plan sponsors of self-insured plans have the right to their own claims data.
- **[TPA] must provide it upon request.** A signed authorization from you as plan fiduciary, along with a standard Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for HIPAA compliance, is all that's required.

### What Raw Claims Data Enables

| Layer | What It Checks |
|-------|---------------|
| Billing Integrity | Duplicate charges, unbundled procedures, upcoding |
| Price Variance | Charges vs. Medicare rates, geographic variance |
| Network Integrity | Surprise billing, balance billing |
| Medical Necessity | Appropriate care setting, frequency |
| Plan Compliance | Cost sharing accuracy, prior authorization |
| Episode Analysis | Cost outliers, fragmented care |
| Pattern Detection | Provider anomalies, rising trends |
